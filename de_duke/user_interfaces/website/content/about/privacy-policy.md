# De-Duke Real Estate Company Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 01/04/2026

At De-Duke Real Estate Company (“De-Duke,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), protecting
your personal information is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we
collect, use, disclose, and safeguard the personal information of our users
across Nigeria and West Africa when using our platform, mobile application,
and services (the “Platform”).
1. What Is Personal Information?
“Personal information” refers to any information that relates to, is associated
with, or can reasonably be linked to an identified or identifiable individual. This
may include contact details, identity documents, financial information, and
other data you provide or that we collect automatically.
2. Who Controls Your Personal Information?
2.1 Controller: When this Policy mentions “De-Duke,” “we,” or “our,” it
refers to the company responsible for your personal information under
this Privacy Policy.
2.2 Payment Controller: If you use our payment services, your personal
information may also be shared with our payment service providers,
who act as Controllers for information related to financial transactions.
3. Personal Information We Collect
3.1 Information Needed to Use the Platform: We collect personal
information to provide services effectively, including:
a) Contact & Profile Information: Name, phone number, email, profile
photo, address.
b) Identity Verification: Government-issued ID, passport, national ID, or
other verification documents.
c) Payment Information: Bank accounts, card details, transaction history.
d) Property and Insurance Information: For users listing properties or
purchasing insurance, we may collect license or insurance information,
business registration, and property details.
e) Experience & Background: Professional experience, online profiles,
and relevant certifications.
3.2 Information You Choose to Provide:
You may provide additional information such as:
a) Gender, language, location, education, or personal preferences.
b) Information about other individuals (with their consent).
c) Photos, videos, reviews, or other content you submit.
d) Communications with us for support purposes.
3.3 Information Collected Automatically:
We may automatically collect:
a) Device and Usage Data: IP address, device type, operating system,
browsing activity on the Platform.
b) Location Data: GPS or IP-based location to facilitate services.
c) Cookies & Similar Technologies: To improve user experience and
Platform performance.
3.4 Information from Third Parties
We may obtain information from third-party sources such as:
a) Payment providers, financial institutions, or insurance companies.
b) Background check and license verification providers.
c) Corporate affiliates or partners.
d) Publicly available information, including social media profiles or
professional websites.
4. How We Use Personal Information
We use personal information to:
a) Provide and Maintain Our Services: Enable you to access the Platform, list
or book properties, make payments, and communicate with other users.
b) Improve and Personalize the Platform: Conduct analytics, develop AI
features, improve services, and customize your experience.
c) Promote Trust and Safety: Verify identity, prevent fraud, enforce policies,
and comply with legal obligations.
d) Advertising and Marketing: Send promotional messages, manage referrals,
and personalize advertisements.
e) Payments and Financial Services: Process payments, prevent financial
fraud, comply with regulations, and offer installment or financing options.
f) Insurance Services: Facilitate purchase and administration of guest or
property insurance if applicable.
Automated processes may be used to detect potential risks or unauthorized
activity on the Platform.
5. Who We Share Personal Information With?
We may share your personal information with:
a) Other Users: To facilitate property transactions, bookings, or
interactions.
b) Service Providers and Partners: For support services, property
management, payments, background checks, and advertising.
c) Government Authorities: To comply with legal requirements, including
tax or licensing obligations.
d) Corporate Affiliates: For business operations, technical support, and
Platform improvement.
e) During Business Transactions: In the event of mergers, acquisitions, or
sales of assets.
We ensure that third parties accessing your personal information are
contractually bound to maintain its confidentiality and security.
6. Why We Share Your Personal Information
a) With Your Consent: When you authorize us to share your information
with third parties.
b) Legal Compliance: To respond to legal requests, prevent harm, or
enforce our rights.
c) Business Transactions: During mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales.
d) Public Profiles: Information you make public may be visible on search
engines or the Platform.
7. Third-Party Partners and Integrations
You may link your account with third-party services. Information shared
through these integrations will be subject to the third party’s privacy policy.
Examples include:
a) Payment providers
b) Social networks
c) Service management or booking tools
8. Changing Your Country of Residence
Your rights and applicable controllers may vary depending on your country of
residence. If you change your country, we may transfer your personal
information to the new relevant controller while continuing to protect it
according to this Policy.
9. Your Rights
Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:
a) Access, update, or delete your personal information.
b) Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.
c) Withdraw consent for processing where applicable.
d) Receive your data in a portable format.
You can manage many of these preferences directly through your account
settings or by contacting us.
10. Security of Personal Information
We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical safeguards
to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure,
alteration, or destruction.
11. Updates to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will be
posted on the Platform, and the effective date will be revised accordingly.
Users are encouraged to review this Policy regularly.

15. Retention of Personal Information
15.1 Retention Periods
We retain your personal information only for as long as it is necessary to fulfill
the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Factors we
consider when determining retention periods include:
a) The duration of your ongoing relationship with De-Duke, including the
time you maintain an account or use our Platform, and the period
afterward during which we may need your data for business
management, dispute resolution, or to defend claims.
b) Legal, regulatory, tax, or auditing requirements that may obligate us to
retain your personal information, such as anti-money laundering and
transaction recordkeeping obligations.
c) Circumstances where retention is necessary to protect the safety of
individuals or our legal position, including compliance with statutes of
limitation, regulatory investigations, or potential litigation.
15.2 Shared Personal Information
Information you share with other users, such as reviews, forum posts, or
comments, may remain visible to others even after your De-Duke account is
deleted.
15.3 Residual Copies
Residual copies of your personal information may remain in backup systems
for a limited period due to security and data protection measures.
16. Security and Privacy-Enhancing Technologies
While no system can be entirely secure, we implement ongoing
administrative, technical, and physical measures to safeguard your personal
information against unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or alteration.
When your personal information is used to improve AI or other analytical tools,
we take extra precautions such as de-identifying data, pseudonymization,
encryption, and restricting access to authorized personnel bound by strict
confidentiality obligations.
17. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in compliance with
applicable law. We will post any revised version on our Platform and update
the “Last Updated” date.
For material changes, we will notify you via email at least thirty (30) days
before the changes take effect. If you do not agree with the revised Privacy
Policy, you may close your account. Continued use of the De-Duke Platform
after the effective date indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy
Policy.
18. Definitions
Any undefined terms in this Privacy Policy have the same meaning as defined
in our Terms of Service or other agreements related to the Platform.
